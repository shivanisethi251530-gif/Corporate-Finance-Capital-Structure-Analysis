# Sources of Funds & Capital Structure Analysis

### A Comparative Financial Study of Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd., Mahindra & Mahindra Ltd. and Maruti Suzuki India Ltd.

**Course:** FINM542 – Corporate Finance I  
**Programme:** MBA – Business Analytics  
**University:** Lovely Professional University  
**Study Period:** FY 2024–25 and FY 2025–26

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project examines how three major Indian automobile manufacturers finance their operations, investments and business growth.

The study focuses on understanding their capital structures, comparing their dependence on debt and equity, and evaluating how different business strategies influence financial decisions.

The analysis uses financial information from annual reports and other secondary sources.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand the major sources of funds used by the selected companies.
- Analyse equity, reserves, borrowings and other financing sources.
- Calculate and compare important capital structure ratios.
- Evaluate financial leverage and financing risks.
- Connect capital structure theories with real-world business decisions.

## 🏢 Companies Analysed

1. Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd. – Focus Company
2. Mahindra & Mahindra Ltd. – Competitor Benchmark
3. Maruti Suzuki India Ltd. – Competitor Benchmark

## 📊 Financial Analysis Performed

The project covers:

- Debt-Equity Ratio
- Debt Ratio
- Equity Ratio
- Long-Term Debt to Capitalisation
- Financial Leverage
- Comparative Financial Analysis
- Historical Trend Analysis

## 🔍 Key Findings

| Financial Metric (FY26) | Tata Motors PV | Mahindra & Mahindra | Maruti Suzuki |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Debt-Equity Ratio | 0.59 | 0.01 | 0.00 |
| Debt Ratio | 0.18 | 0.01 | 0.00 |
| Equity Ratio | 0.31 | 0.62 | 0.72 |
| Financial Leverage | 3.21x | 1.62x | 1.39x |

**Major Observations:**

- Tata Motors PV demonstrates relatively higher financial leverage, reflecting its capital-intensive business and investment requirements.
- Mahindra & Mahindra maintains a debt-light financing structure in its standalone automotive and farm operations.
- Maruti Suzuki demonstrates a highly conservative financing approach, with virtually zero borrowings.
- All three companies rely on trade payables as an important source of working capital financing.

## 📚 Theoretical Framework

The study connects financial findings with important capital structure theories:

- Modigliani and Miller Theory
- Pecking Order Theory
- Trade-off Theory
- Agency Theory
